# Third-party notices

Semantic Gaussian Wrapping is an independent research implementation whose
public layout and mathematical parameterization follow the original 3D
Gaussian Splatting project.  The CUDA rasterizer contains derivative portions
from the Inria/MPII `diff-gaussian-rasterization` implementation and therefore
remains subject to the research-only terms in `LICENSE.md`; the corresponding
source files retain their copyright headers.

The design also builds on ideas described by Gaussian Wrapping, Gaga,
RaDe-GS, Mip-Splatting, Gaussian Opacity Fields, and PlanarGS.  No pretrained
weights from those projects are redistributed here.
